## Current save point

Created a first working browser prototype with:

- Mountain-bike and moto-cross riding modes.
- Beginner, intermediate, and advanced route choices.
- A map-style preview with route details.
- A safety reminder about maps, helmets, water, and sharing a ride plan.
- Offline-ready messaging; this prototype uses sample route data and does not yet connect to live GPS or a map service.
